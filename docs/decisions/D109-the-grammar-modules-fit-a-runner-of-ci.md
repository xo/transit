# D109. The grammar modules fit a runner of CI

Status: Decided.

On 2026-10-02, CI failed on commit `0b3e678` when the runner killed two jobs
for want of memory. A runner of GitHub has 16 GB. The generated `parser.go`
of `grammars/postgres` is 31 MB, and three checks need more than a runner
has:

- The generator test of postgres needs 11.6 GB, and more than twice that
  with `-race`.
- A lint of the test module, which imports postgres, needs 15 GB when the
  cache is cold, and 9 GB with `GOGC=25` and one job at a time.
- A lint of `grammars/postgres` needs 17.5 GB with every linter. The
  linters `dupl` (11.7 GB alone), `goconst` (10.1 GB) and `gosec` (8.3 GB)
  take the most, and with those three off it needs 12 GB.

Ken decided on 2026-10-02, answering question 80, to find the heavy linters
and turn them off for the grammar modules only:

1. The grammar modules are linted without `dupl`, `goconst` and `gosec`.
   They find nothing in generated tables, and a hand-written scanner keeps
   every other linter.
2. The lint jobs run with `GOGC=25` and one job at a time
   (`--concurrency 1`).
3. The generator tests of the grammar modules run without `-race`, with
   `GOGC=25`. Every other test of a grammar module still runs with `-race`.
   The race detector adds nothing to a test that compares generated bytes.

"Before you stage" in `AGENTS.md` and the workflow of CI run the same
commands.
